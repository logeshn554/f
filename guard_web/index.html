What the simulation assumes

    Candle Paths v4

    Deterministic market calculations. No keys, no LLM configuration, no exchange orders.

    01 · DIRECTION

    Read candle geometry

    Measure bodies, upper and lower wicks, gaps, movement over multiple candle spans, range expansion, and path efficiency. No moving-average or RSI entry rule is used.

    02 · ENTRY

    Compare future paths

    Compare every eligible historical context in a rolling window. Keep up to 64 separated similar contexts whose subsequent eight candles are already known. Rescale those paths to current volatility, then compare BUY, SELL and WAIT after costs.

    03 · EXITS

    Recalculate take-profit and stop-loss

    Evaluate up to 18 direction/TP/SL combinations using weighted path outcomes. Select the plan with the strongest net edge after uncertainty and tail-loss penalties. Replan each completed candle; stops never widen and targets stay capped at four initial risk units. Close if the current direction loses its estimated edge.

    Integer contract sizing can make a trade too large for this balance. Such trades are skipped. Gaps and slippage can exceed planned losses.

    Funding is a conservative estimate, not actual exchange settlement. The USD ledger excludes INR conversion and tax on profits. Exits assume available liquidity.

    Operational safeguards

    SQLite persists positions and the fill journal. Only one service can own this account. Stale quotes, invalid candles, suspended markets and changed contract terms block fills. Polling retries with backoff, and the interface flags stale data.

    Pause stops new entries while managing open positions. Close paper position queues a simulated exit at the next valid quote and pauses entries. If the feed is unavailable, it waits. Restart preserves state and risk halts. Five-second polling can miss intrapoll price excursions.

    This is a local paper-trading system, not a certified production trading platform. Long-duration forward testing, true funding, depth-aware fills, reconciliation and operational review remain necessary for any capital deployment.
